# AI OS Map

![[AI OS Map.jpg]]

How the system fits together: every AI reads and writes the same memory (this folder), skills are the playbooks they follow, loops run on a schedule and bring the results to you, and your corrections become new rules in CLAUDE.md.

Edit it: open `AI OS Map.excalidraw` at [excalidraw.com](https://excalidraw.com) (File, Open) or install the Excalidraw community plugin in Obsidian. Then export a new image with the same name.

Make your own: ask your AI "Draw my AI OS as an Excalidraw diagram: my AIs on the left, my memory in the middle, my life areas on the right, my loops below. Save it as Diagrams/My AI OS.excalidraw."
