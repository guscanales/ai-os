# Reflection Prompts

1. What made the week feel easier?
2. What did I avoid, and what was the smallest useful step?
3. Which number changed enough to deserve attention?
4. What can I stop doing next week?
5. Did my time match my values?
6. Who would appreciate a simple check-in?

Answer one prompt at a time. The AI should ask a useful question rather than diagnose a feeling.
