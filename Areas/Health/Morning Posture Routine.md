# Morning Posture Routine

Fictional trainer routine. About 11 minutes. Move gently and stop if symptoms increase.

| Move | Time | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Relaxed breathing | 1 minute | Let shoulders settle |
| Chin tucks | 2 minutes | Small motion, no forced stretch |
| Wall slides | 2 minutes | Keep ribs comfortable |
| Thoracic rotation | 2 minutes | Turn from upper back |
| Doorway chest stretch | 2 minutes | Gentle tension |
| Band pull-aparts | 2 minutes | Light band, controlled reps |

This is a reference routine, not another task list. Persistent neck pain goes to a qualified professional.
