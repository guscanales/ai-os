## Practice problem

Design a search assistant over internal policy notes. Explain ingestion, retrieval, grounded answers and what happens when the evidence is missing.

| Topic | Evidence to prepare |
|---|---|
| Retrieval | Compare keyword and embedding search on 30 questions |
| Evaluation | Measure answer support and retrieval recall separately |
| Reliability | Timeouts, fallback response and versioned prompts |
| Privacy | Limit source access before retrieval |
| Tradeoff | Start simple before adding another model |

## Practice outline

Spend 5 minutes clarifying the user. Draw the data path in words. Spend 10 minutes on evaluation and failure cases. Finish with cost and latency limits.

## Questions for the team
